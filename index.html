What is Fastlane v2?

Fastlane is the AI that replaces your creative marketing agency. Senna, its agent, learns your brand from your website, studies your competitors and your market, builds campaigns, creates the ads and organic content, launches them across Meta, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, then reads the results and feeds them into the next batch. Every lap makes the next one smarter.

Does it actually replace my agency?

Senna handles the research, creative production, launch and optimization loop. Your team stays in control: you review concepts and approve campaigns before they go live.

How much control do I have?

You approve the creative and campaign before launch. Senna gives you a clear view of what is working and recommends what to make next.

Which channels does it launch on?

Fastlane supports Meta ads and organic publishing to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Who is it for?

Fastlane is built for founders, solopreneurs and growth teams who want to make and test more creative without adding an agency-sized team.

Is Fastlane the same as the fastlane iOS build tool?

No. This is Fastlane, the AI marketing platform for creating, launching and learning from ads.
